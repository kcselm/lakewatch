# Water Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Make the lake's water hold up at binocular and telescope range: depth from the terrain, see-through shallows, a soft shoreline and a wet band, planar reflections of the scene and the birds, a contact line and a V wake for every bird in the water, rings that bend the surface, ripples that don't shimmer with distance, and calm patches that drift downwind with the gusts.

**Architecture:**
- **Pure logic, tested in node (Part 1):** a water-depth bake from the rendered terrain and its alpha; a wet band with a GLSL twin, drawn per fragment on the terrain; the Kelvin wake as a function with a GLSL twin; ring shapes from the research; a tracker of the birds in the water; Fresnel and the reflection offset; a Water reflections setting. The swimmers stop ringing a wake; the ring cap rises to 64.
- **The water shader and the mirror (Part 2):** the shader rebuilt around the depth texture, a half-size mirrored render of the scene, the floaters' contact lines and wakes, rings as waves, ripple octaves that fade by pixel footprint, and a calm-patch field sharing the reeds' gust front. Checked by eye against the baseline captures.

**Tech Stack:** Vite 8, TypeScript 7, Vitest 5 (`environment: 'node'`), Three.js 0.186, Preact.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-30-water-design.md`. Read it first.
- Every number comes from `docs/research/2026-09-30-lake-water.md`: section 9 holds the numbers for the code and the test thresholds, section 10 the photo references.
- The baseline captures are in `docs/research/water-baseline/`; Part 2's before-and-after pairs go beside them.

## Decisions made while planning

- **The swimmers' wakes are no longer rings** (Task 3). The V is drawn from each bird's measured speed (Task 5, Task 9), so the dabbler, shoveler, coot, and grebe settings lose `wake`, and the swim tests that counted wake rings now check that a cruising Mallard rings the water only when its bill or body goes in, and swims fast enough for a full V. The spec had the rings kept and the V drawn as well; with the research's lifetimes that would overflow any ring cap the uniforms can afford.
- **The ring cap is 64,** from 32 (Task 4), and the ring budget test's expected mean moves from 10–18 to 20–36.
- **The wet band is drawn per fragment on the terrain,** not in `terrainColor` (Task 2). A 10 cm band on the mesh's 2 m grid would smear across whole cells as a vertex colour. `wetDarkening` is a pure function with a GLSL twin; the terrain carries each vertex's gravel share as `aGravel` and darkens by each fragment's own height in `onBeforeCompile`. `terrainColor`'s result is unchanged.
- **The depth texture is half-float** (Task 7): WebGL 2 filters 16-bit floats linearly without an extension, and 32-bit floats only with `OES_texture_float_linear`. `bakeWaterDepth` returns float32; Task 7 converts with `THREE.DataUtils.toHalfFloat`. `WaterDepth.at` filters bilinearly, as the GPU will.
- **Floaters are found from the registry,** not reported by the behaviours (Task 5): shown, no higher than 2 cm above the surface, and over water. Their velocity is measured from their steps and smoothed over 0.3 s. `wading` is anything in the water whose behaviour isn't `swim`.
- **The reflections setting keeps `version: 1`** (Task 6): settings saved before it have none and take the default, on.
- **Grass and the zone outlines stay out of the mirror by layer** (Task 8): they move to layer 1, the main camera sees layers 0 and 1, the mirror only 0. Nothing else has to opt in.
- **The gust front moves at the wind's speed** (Task 11): its ω drops from 2.2 to 1.0 rad/s, shared by the reeds and the water, after the research found the reeds' front running at 6.3 m/s against a 2.8 m/s wind. The reeds are checked by eye; if they look worse, both keep 2.2.

## Global Constraints

- **Frame rate:** 60 fps on an integrated GPU in Chrome with reflections on and every bird on screen. If the mirror costs more, the fallbacks in order: a quarter-size target, the reeds left out of it, reflections off by default.
- **Art direction:** field-guide plate. The water is a flat plane with shaded normals; no wave geometry, refraction, caustics, or splashes (spec, Out of scope).
- **Interfaces:** `BirdModel`, `PoseState`, and `BehaviorContext.onRipple` do not change. `RippleBuffer`'s API does not change; only its lifetimes and cap.
- **Existing birds unchanged:** no species file, atlas, or pose changes; the fingerprints hold at every task.
- **Units:** metres and seconds; radians in code, degrees only in comments and the research.
- **Tests:** Vitest runs in `environment: 'node'`: no DOM, no WebGL. Rendering is checked by eye, with Playwright screenshots where a step asks (the framings of `docs/research/water-baseline/`).
- **Commits:** every commit leaves `npm test` green. Never commit `node_modules`, `.env`, `dist`, `data/raw`, `.wrangler`, or `.playwright-mcp`.
- **Worktrees:** `npm ci` in each worktree, never a symlink or junction; **never `git stash`**: the stash is shared by every worktree.
- **Line endings:** the repo's files are CRLF in the working copy (`core.autocrlf` is true). The patches below are LF; apply them with `git apply --ignore-whitespace`, which the replay used.

+import type { Heightfield } from './heightfield'
+
+/** [x0, x1, z0, z1]: the basin, the bench, and the shelf, with a margin of bank all round */
+export const WATER_DEPTH_BOUNDS = [-44, 48, -70, -2] as const
+const TEXEL = 0.1
+
+/**
+ * How deep the water's colour builds: alpha is 1 - exp(-depth / VISIBILITY). 0.06 m from a Secchi depth of 0.7 m seen
+ * at the vantage's grazing angles (research, 2026-09-30-lake-water.md section 2), so the bed is gone by 0.3 m.
+ */
+export const VISIBILITY = 0.06
+
+export function waterAlpha(depth: number): number {
+  return depth <= 0 ? 0 : 1 - Math.exp(-depth / VISIBILITY)
+}
+
+export interface WaterDepth {
+  /** depth in metres, row by row from z0, 0 on land */
+  data: Float32Array
+  width: number
+  height: number
+  bounds: typeof WATER_DEPTH_BOUNDS
+  /** the depth at (x, z), filtered between texels as the shader reads it, clamped to the bounds */
+  at(x: number, z: number): number
+}
+
+/**
+ * The water's depth over the basin, baked once from the rendered terrain (`terrainSurface`), so the water's colour,
+ * clarity, and shoreline meet the ground the player sees. The water's surface is y = 0.
+ */
+export function bakeWaterDepth(surface: Heightfield): WaterDepth {
+  const [x0, x1, z0, z1] = WATER_DEPTH_BOUNDS
+  const width = Math.round((x1 - x0) / TEXEL), height = Math.round((z1 - z0) / TEXEL)
+  const data = new Float32Array(width * height)
+  for (let j = 0; j < height; j++) {
+    const z = z0 + (j + 0.5) * TEXEL
+    for (let i = 0; i < width; i++) data[j * width + i] = Math.max(0, -surface.sample(x0 + (i + 0.5) * TEXEL, z))
+  }
+  return {
+    data, width, height, bounds: WATER_DEPTH_BOUNDS,
+    at(x, z) {
+      // between the four nearest texel centres, as the GPU's linear filter reads the texture
+      const gx = Math.min(width - 1, Math.max(0, (x - x0) / TEXEL - 0.5))
+      const gz = Math.min(height - 1, Math.max(0, (z - z0) / TEXEL - 0.5))
+      const i = Math.min(width - 2, Math.floor(gx)), j = Math.min(height - 2, Math.floor(gz))
+      const u = gx - i, v = gz - j
+      const d = (a: number, b: number) => data[b * width + a]!
+      return (d(i, j) * (1 - u) + d(i + 1, j) * u) * (1 - v) + (d(i, j + 1) * (1 - u) + d(i + 1, j + 1) * u) * v
+    },
+  }
+}

+/**
+ * A swimming bird's V (research 2026-09-30-lake-water.md section 5). A duck at 0.35-0.70 m/s is in the Kelvin regime:
+ * the wake's outer edge is at 19.47 degrees either side of its track, its brightest arms a little inside, about 15
+ * degrees. Nothing slower than c_min, 0.23 m/s, makes waves; the wake comes in over 0.23-0.30 m/s. It fades behind the
+ * bird with an e-folding distance of 1.5 m, and is drawn out to 5 m.
+ */
+export const WAKE = {
+  minSpeed: 0.23,
+  fullSpeed: 0.30,
+  halfAngle: 19.47 * Math.PI / 180,
+  armAngle: 15 * Math.PI / 180,
+  efold: 1.5,
+  reach: 5,
+  /** the wake's share on its centreline, between the arms, from its transverse crests */
+  centre: 0.4,
+} as const
+
+const G = 9.81
+
+function smooth(t: number): number {
+  const c = Math.min(1, Math.max(0, t))
+  return c * c * (3 - 2 * c)
+}
+
+/** how much of a wake a bird swimming at `speed` m/s makes, 0..1 */
+export function wakeStrength(speed: number): number {
+  return speed <= WAKE.minSpeed ? 0 : smooth((speed - WAKE.minSpeed) / (WAKE.fullSpeed - WAKE.minSpeed))
+}
+
+/** the wavelength of the crests across the track behind a bird at `speed`: where a wave's speed matches the bird's */
+export function transverseWavelength(speed: number): number {
+  return 2 * Math.PI * speed * speed / G
+}
+
+/**
+ * The wake's height envelope, 0..1, at a point `behind` metres back along the bird's track and `across` metres to
+ * either side, for a bird at `speed`: 0 outside the wedge, ahead of the bird, and past its reach.
+ */
+export function wakeAt(behind: number, across: number, speed: number): number {
+  if (behind <= 0 || behind > WAKE.reach) return 0
+  const a = Math.atan2(Math.abs(across), behind)
+  if (a >= WAKE.halfAngle) return 0
+  const arm = a >= WAKE.armAngle
+    ? smooth((WAKE.halfAngle - a) / (WAKE.halfAngle - WAKE.armAngle))
+    : WAKE.centre + (1 - WAKE.centre) * smooth(a / WAKE.armAngle)
+  return arm * Math.exp(-behind / WAKE.efold) * wakeStrength(speed)
+}
+
